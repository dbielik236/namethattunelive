More than a trivia night

You don't just listen to the music... you're part of it.

Hosted and performed live by Gus & Jean, Name That Tune brings the excitement of a musical game show into the room. Every song is performed live, every round brings a new challenge, and every team has a chance to take the lead. From the first familiar notes to the final mystery medley, it's a fully immersive musical experience that brings people together through the songs they know and love.

Every round brings something different.

From recognizing a familiar guitar intro to tackling a musical mystery, each round puts a different twist on the songs you know and love.

Round 01

Name That Tune

It starts with the music. Listen to Gus & Jean perform the guitar introduction and see how quickly your team recognizes the song.

Round 02

Finish the Lyric

You know the song. But can you remember what comes next? Listen closely and put your lyrical memory to the test.

Round 03

Musical Mashup

Two songs, unexpected combinations, and one musical performance. Can your team identify what's being played?

Round 04

Name That Decade

Travel through the decades with songs that defined their time. Your challenge? Figure out when they belong.

Final Round

The Mystery Medley

The ultimate musical challenge. Listen as the clues unfold and become increasingly recognizable. Can your team put it all together?
